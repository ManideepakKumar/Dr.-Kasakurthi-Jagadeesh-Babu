Background of Dr. Kasakurthi Jagadeesh Babu:

Dr. Kasakurthi Jagadeesh Babu (1976 – September 20, 2026) was a prominent 50-year-old Indian neurosurgeon who served as the Professor and Head of the Department of Neurosurgery at Mamata Medical College in Khammam, Telangana. He was a highly respected medical professional, a member of the Neurosurgery Society of India, and the former General Secretary of the Indian Medical Association (IMA) in Khammam.

Education: He completed his MBBS and MS in General Surgery from Guntur Medical College and subsequently earned his M.Ch in Neurosurgery from Andhra Medical College in Visakhapatnam.

Expertise: With over 15 years of experience, he specialized in advanced procedures including endoscopic brain and spine surgery, neuro-oncology, skull base surgery, and craniospinal trauma management.
He was also associated with Ushodaya Hospital in Khammam.

Contributions: He authored numerous research papers on spinal surgeries and intracranial pressure management, and frequently organized national continuing medical education (CME) seminars in collaboration with premier institutes like AIIMS and NIMHANS.
